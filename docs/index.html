Boox Calendar

Calendar, reminders and handwritten notes in a single app for Onyx Boox e-ink tablets, synchronised with your Google account.

Download the latest version (APK) Source code on GitHub

What it does

  • Google Calendar with month, week and day views, and quick event creation.
  • Reminders from Google Tasks, also inside the calendar, with lists and due dates.
  • Handwritten notes with the tablet's stylus: quick note of the day, notebook with folders and tags, on-device handwriting recognition and text search.
  • Notes in Google Drive, as editable PDF, in a folder owned by the app. Import PDFs from other apps (OneNote, scans) and search their text.
  • Widgets for the home screen: today, week, agenda, notes of the day and search.
  • Designed for e-ink: black and white, no animations, minimal refreshes.

Install

  1. Download the APK of the latest version from the tablet's browser.
  2. If BooxOS asks, allow the browser to install apps from unknown sources.
  3. Open the app → Settings → Connect my Google account. The browser opens; pick your account and accept the Calendar, Tasks and Drive (app files only) permissions.
  4. Exclude the app from BooxOS power optimisation so it can sync in the background; the app itself reminds you.

Privacy

Your data lives only on your tablet and in your Google account. No third-party servers, no analytics, no advertising. Details in the privacy policy.
